# Pre-registration: P8, fd75d against Triton on the 8-bit cache at depth (logit check)

Registered 2026-11-03 22:11 EST by the stages 2-3 fork, at the ambassador's request. Matt approved the check on 2026-11-02. The first
commit of this file freezes it; later changes are dated amendments appended at the end.

## Why

- **The stage 2 result.** The split cache (8-bit keys, 4-bit values) scored 11.5 points above the 8-bit cache at 64K:
  H2s, 90% interval [+5.2, +18.8] (`testsuite/results/turing/longctx/stage2/report.md`).
- **Two differences, not one.** Besides the value bits, the two arms used different decode kernels:
  - the lab's fd75d reads the 8-bit cache;
  - Triton's split kernel reads the split cache.
- **32K showed no gap.** There, the fd75d-read 8-bit cache and the Triton-read fp16 cache agreed on every item.
- **fd75d is untested end to end at depth.** It was validated end to end only on short prompts (numerics run
  `w11-e47-n50`), and at kernel level against an fp64 reference at 28K.

## Question

On the same 8-bit cache contents, does fd75d's decode attention give the same next-token distributions as Triton's
split kernel at 28K, 48K and 64K?

## Method

- **Engine.** The adopted w13 build, unchanged, with the stage 2 environment: EXL3_MGEMM_N_THRESHOLD=0,
  EXL3_FD75D_LIVE=0, EXL3_INT8_SQ_MAX_M=2, EXL3_GEMV_SK=2.
  - 8-bit cache (`-cq 8,8`), chunk 1024, recurrent checkpoint cache `-rcs 2`.
  - No drafting: these are the main model's logits.
  - The allocator is exllamav3's default, as in stages 1-3.
- **Tool.** `tools/turing/numerics.py`, unchanged; the same tool ran fd75d's earlier end-to-end check.
  - It runs in `--streams` mode, with one pass of its default (served) dispatch per run.
  - `--full-logits-stride 1` gives exact KL at every position; top-k is 20.
- **The only switch.** `EXL3_FD75D=1` selects fd75d, the adopted default. `EXL3_FD75D=0` selects Triton's split kernel
  (exllamav3 `modules/attention_fn/bc_attn.py`, `_fd75d_ok`). Nothing else differs between runs.
- **Runs.** One fresh process each, in this order: F (fd75d), T1 (Triton), T2 (Triton). T1 against T2 is the floor:
  two runs that share the kernel.
- **Streams.** Nine items: multivalue at k = 12, from the stage 2 test seed, indices 0, 1 and 2 on the 96-item depth
  grid.
  - **Lengths:** 28,672 (28K), 49,152 (48K) and 65,536 (64K) tokens, each prompt at most L - 512.
  - **The 64K items** are stage 2's `graded-64k` items 0-2.
  - **The 28K and 48K sets** are built with the same wrapper (`testsuite/p8_longctx/items.py`). Their manifests are
    committed before the run.
- **Prompt.** The item, rendered as TabbyAPI rendered it in stage 2 (P6's no-think chat wrap). It is tokenized on the
  desktop with the model's own tokenizer.
- **Response, teacher-forced one token per forward (the decode path).** The item's 12 gold values, one per line,
  followed by `<|im_end|>`: about 96 positions per item.
- **Prefill.** Each run prefills each prompt in its own process, in numerics' default prefill mode. The prefill path is
  the same in every run, and fd75d enters only the decode-shape forwards.

## Measures

Per length (three items each), pooled over all nine items, and per item:
- **KL.** Exact per-position KL(T1 || F) and KL(T1 || T2): mean, p50, p90, p99 and max.
- **Top-1 agreement** of F with T1, and of T2 with T1.
- **Position.** The KL by answer position (first value, later values), because retrieval happens there.

## Decision rule

fd75d **diverges** from Triton at a length if either holds:
- mean KL(T1 || F) > 2 × mean KL(T1 || T2) + 1e-4 nats;
- top-1 agreement (F, T1) is more than 1 percentage point below top-1 agreement (T2, T1).

What follows:
- **If fd75d diverges at 48K or 64K,** the next step is the accuracy arm,
  `research/prereg/2026-11-03-p8-fd75d-accuracy-arm.md`. It is not queued until the ambassador's gate record exists.
- **If it diverges at neither,** the kernel is ruled out as the source of the H2s gap at the logit level. That is
  reported, and the line stops there.
- **28K is the control,** the length to which fd75d's kernel test reached. Divergence at 28K as well would point to
  something other than depth.

## Predictions (before any data)

- **F1.** fd75d does not diverge at 28K.
- **F2.** fd75d does not diverge at 48K or 64K. This is the null; the check exists because stage 2 raised the
  question.

## Budget and safeguards

- **Budget:** at most 1 GPU hour; the estimate is about 41 minutes. Each run takes about 12 minutes of prefill for
  the nine prompts, about a minute of teacher-forced decode, and a model load. A run that would push the total past
  60 minutes is not started, and the report says so.
- **Queue and power:** one desktop queue step, with the guard on and the power limit checked at 260 W without sudo.
- **Leave alone:** nothing goes on the saved TabbyAPI default, and the other project's processes are not touched.
- **Files:** outputs go to `$L/runs/longctx/fd75d/`, and nothing is deleted.

## Not covered

- Accuracy: the separate accuracy-arm registration.
- Prefill kernels.
- Formats other than the 8-bit cache.

## Amendment 1 (2026-11-03 23:40 EST, by the stages 2-3 fork): where the analysis ran (implementation only)

The measures and the decision rule do not change.

- **The GPU runs went as registered.** F, T1 and T2 ran in the queue step `x-p8lc-fd75d` and finished at 22:28,
  22:41 and 22:54 EST: 773, 786 and 788 s, 39.1 minutes in all, within the hour.
- **The analysis could not finish on the desktop.**
  - The queue step's analysis loaded all three runs' full logits at once, about 1.3 GB plus float64 temporaries,
    while other processes held most of the desktop's memory.
  - The guard stopped it 17 times for a memory stall, from 22:57 to 23:38. The queue retried each time, because
    guard stops do not count as tries.
  - At 23:38 the step was withdrawn: queue rev 50 removes it, and our queue unit was stopped. Nothing else on the
    desktop was touched.
- **The analysis ran on the laptop instead, off-queue, under nice.**
  - It read copies of the three run files and the streams; their SHA-256 matched the desktop's.
  - `fd75d_analyze.py` now opens run files lazily and computes the exact KL per item, 16 positions at a time. Its
    peak resident memory was 1.5 GB.
  - The 37 CPU tests, including the synthetic checks of the rule, pass in zarael-sandbox before and after that change.
